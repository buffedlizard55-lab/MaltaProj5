What the data shows — and what it cannot

  • It is a festival city with a thin weekly layer. 135 annual entries against 25 weekly ones. The weekly layer is mostly farmers markets, museum nights and standing park programmes.
  • Blank is the honest default. 118 rows carry review notes and 186 have at least one blank tracked field. Where an organizer has not published a next date, the date cell is blank rather than extrapolated — a recurring pattern is not a confirmed future instance.
  • Many entries record 2026 editions that have already taken place (for example Tulip Day in March, the Opera Ball gala and the July 4 fireworks). They are retained as dated records and are not presented as upcoming; the register has no “upcoming only” filter because that would require re-deriving dates the organizers have not published.
  • There is no citywide denominator. No official source publishes a complete count of San Francisco events, so this register cannot claim exhaustive coverage. Known gaps: library programmes, most theatre, neighbourhood block parties, sports beyond the Giants, and weekday civic meetings.
  • Two registers were reconciled. The 86-row register (sessions 3a19e72d/5a85baf2) and the 167-row inventory (session 4f47c142) were merged with an explicit map; overlapping rows were reconciled rather than counted twice.
