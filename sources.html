Sources & methodology

Every inventory row links to official pages. This ledger records what was checked, the supporting excerpts, and what remains unresolved.

Verification rules

  1. Only organizer, venue, museum or municipal pages supply admitted facts. Third-party calendars were used for discovery only, never as row evidence.
  2. Read official pages on 18 October 2026; transcribe supported event names, schedules, times, venues and admission restrictions. Keep excerpts below for audit. Excerpts are selected transcriptions, not complete archived pages.
  3. Weekly, monthly and annual classifications require explicit recurrence language. Special is an editorial bucket for dated programs when recurrence is not established; it does not mean one-time only.
  4. Preserve dates as published. Do not generate next occurrences or assign years from neighboring listings. Do not call a recurring pattern a confirmed future instance.
  5. Only San Francisco venues are admitted. Candidates with uncertain city boundaries stay in review. No nearby Bay Area cities or online-only programs are included.
  6. Blank schedule, time, venue or admission fields are flagged unverified. A source link verifies only the facts it actually states. A blank admission field never means free.

Analysis & limitations

This snapshot is not a full list of all SF events. There is no citywide completeness denominator. Museum and market programs are overrepresented; sports, library programs, theatre, civic meetings, nightlife and small neighborhood events are undercovered. No claim is made about popularity, attendance, total event counts citywide, or current ticket availability.

Eight After Dark theme rows are occurrences of one weekly series. Counts distinguish inventory rows from series/standalone entries. One theme is explicitly annual and is classified accordingly. Annual and special categories therefore describe both series and editions; do not sum counts as unique gatherings.

No automatic fact-verification or ongoing refresh is claimed. CI checks data structure, evidence references, and local links; it cannot establish factual truth. Official pages were successfully read at research time except the two explicitly failed candidates. External availability may change; it is not continuously monitored.
