What the register can tell us

251 candidate entries were assessed. 172 meet the directory’s name, venue and SF-geography gate; 79 are held for review. These are entries, not unique events: 14 listed programme umbrellas overlap their dated children. This is not a random sample of San Francisco activity.

Counts describe this dataset only. They cannot establish citywide event frequency, neighborhood equity, attendance, economic impact, or that one category dominates the city.

Biases and limits

  • Official web calendars favor larger, web-visible institutions; small community, faith, nightlife and pop-up gatherings are underrepresented.
  • Blocked and JavaScript-only pages reduce readable evidence. Their candidates are not proof of nonexistence and are not silently counted as verified.
  • Regional festivals need venue-level separation. A San Francisco brand does not place every performance inside the city.
  • Domain counts are not counts of organizers. A .org suffix is not evidence of nonprofit legal status; no such inference is made.
  • Snapshot dates are not live ticket availability, cancellations or a guarantee that a source has not changed.

Next research priorities

Isolate SF-only regional screenings; verify individual sports fixtures, theater/music performances, civic and faith calendars; resolve conflicting hours/street extents; revisit unreadable primary pages. Coverage is intentionally described as incomplete.
