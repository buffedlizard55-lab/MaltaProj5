Key findings

  1. The calendar peaks in “Second Summer.” Annual events cluster in May–October (52 of 95 annual events fall in those months). September and October alone host 20 annual events, including Hardly Strictly Bluegrass, Fleet Week, Folsom Street Fair and the Italian Heritage Parade.
  2. Weekly life is anchored by markets and museum nights. San Francisco runs 13 weekly farmers markets year-round, operated mainly by three organizations (Foodwise, PCFMA and the Agricultural Institute of Marin), plus recurring weekly museum nights (Cal Academy NightLife, Exploratorium After Dark) and the Golden Gate Park Band, which has played free Sunday concerts since 1882.
  3. Free access is the norm. 101 of 167 listed events (60%) are free to attend or free with donations — including the city’s largest festivals (Hardly Strictly Bluegrass, Stern Grove, Pride, Carnaval, Fleet Week, Sunday Streets).
  4. Verification status. 147 of 167 rows are fully verified against official sources; 20 rows carry flags (typically a date not yet announced, a missing organizer website, or a detail sourced only from the official tourism body’s guide). Every row links to an official source; values that could not be confirmed were left blank — never inferred.
  5. 2026 was a one-off-heavy year. Special 2026-only events included the Red Bull Showrun F1 street demo (Feb 21), Fourth of July fireworks launched from the Golden Gate Bridge towers for America’s 250th birthday (Jul 4), the Pokemon World Championships at Moscone (Aug 28–30) and Super Bowl LX fan events (the game itself was played in Santa Clara).
  6. Look-ahead is short. As of the snapshot date, only 2 events had officially announced 2027 dates (SF Pride, June 26–27, 2027; Cherry Blossom Festival, April 10–11 and 17–18, 2027). Most organizers announce next-year dates 3–9 months out.
  7. Out-of-city listings are common in “San Francisco” calendars. Events at the Cow Palace (Daly City), PayPal Park (San Jose), Levi’s Stadium (Santa Clara), Shoreline-adjacent festivals and even Sonoma and Mill Valley film festivals appear in SF-branded guides. This dataset excludes them and logs each exclusion on the Flagged for Review page.

Method & scope

  • Geography: events must take place inside San Francisco city limits. Notable nearby events at out-of-city venues (Cow Palace/Daly City, PayPal Park/San Jose, Levi’s Stadium/Santa Clara, and others) are excluded and logged.
  • Sourcing: each row links to an official site — the organizer’s or venue’s own website, a City of San Francisco site (sf.gov, SFMTA, SF Environment, Port of SF, Presidio Trust), or SF Travel, the City’s official destination marketing organization. Aggregators and media articles are never the source of record.
  • Verification: every row was checked against its linked source on 2026-10-18. If a value (typically a not-yet-announced date) could not be confirmed, it was left blank and the row is flagged on the Flagged for Review page. Nothing is inferred.
  • Editorial scope: professional sports league schedules and trade conventions are excluded as categories (individual special events at those venues are included).
  • Coordination: research, verification and integration are logged in COORDINATION.md in the repository.
