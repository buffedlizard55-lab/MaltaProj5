3 · Notable irregularities found during verification

  • Stale or recycled event info: a third-party guide claimed an “Opera at the Ballpark — La Traviata, Nov 11, 2026” at Oracle Park. The official SF Opera 2026–27 season contains no La Traviata, and the official simulcast page still shows the Nov 11, 2022 edition. The claim was rejected and logged (see exclusion log).
  • Ambiguous race date: The Giant Race (Oracle Park) has no clearly dated 2026 edition: SF Travel lists it undated, and the Giants’ events page shows “August 17–18”, which matches the 2024 edition. Date left unverified.
  • SF-branded guides list out-of-city events: SF Travel’s monthly guides include events in Daly City (Cow Palace), Sonoma, Mill Valley, Half Moon Bay, Woodside and San Jose under “San Francisco” events. All are excluded here and logged.
  • Date moved: SF Opera’s Opera Ball 2026 was moved from Sep 12 to Nov 12, 2026 per the Opera’s announcement — both dates appear in different listings.
  • TBD holiday dates: as of the snapshot, 2026 lighting-ceremony dates for Union Square and Ghirardelli Square were listed as “TBD” by SF Travel; Embarcadero Center’s official site had published Nov 18, 2026 (40th annual).
  • Hours discrepancy: SF Travel lists Ferry Plaza Farmers Market Tuesday hours as 8am; the operator (Foodwise) lists 10am–2pm. The operator’s value is used.
  • New Year’s Eve fireworks: the annual Embarcadero midnight fireworks are documented by the Port of San Francisco for Dec 31, 2025; the 2026/27 edition had not been published on official City pages at snapshot time.
