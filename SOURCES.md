# SOURCES.md — official source register

Every event row in `data/events.json` cites one of the pages below. All were
retrieved on **2026-10-18**. Nothing in the dataset is sourced from an
aggregator calendar (Funcheap, SF Station, Timeout, Eventbrite, Wikipedia, news
outlets, hotel/travel blogs); those were used only to *discover* candidate events,
and `tools/validate.py` fails the build if any of them appears as a row's source.

- 61 event rows
- 51 unique official pages
- 43 distinct official domains
