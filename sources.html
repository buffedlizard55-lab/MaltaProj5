Sources & method

Every row links to official pages. This ledger records what was checked, the supporting excerpts, and what remains unresolved.

Verification rules

  1. Only organizer, venue, museum or municipal pages supply admitted facts. Tourism-board guides and third-party calendars were used for discovery only, never as the sole row evidence.
  2. Official pages were read on 18 October 2026 and the supported event names, schedules, times, venues and admission restrictions transcribed. Excerpts below are selected transcriptions, not complete archived pages.
  3. Weekly, monthly and annual classifications require explicit recurrence language. Special is an editorial bucket for dated programmes; it does not mean one-time only.
  4. Dates are preserved as published. Next occurrences are not generated from a pattern, and a recurring pattern is not presented as a confirmed future instance.
  5. Only San Francisco venues are admitted. Events at Levi's Stadium, the Cow Palace and other out-of-city venues are excluded and recorded on the review page. No nearby Bay Area cities or online-only programmes.
  6. Blank schedule, time, venue or admission cells are flagged unverified. A source link verifies only the facts it states; a blank admission cell never means free.
  7. Automated re-check: every cited URL is fetched in CI and its text searched for the event name. Pages that block automated clients are reported, not treated as verified. Automation cannot prove a date or price.

Analysis & limitations

This snapshot is not a complete list of all San Francisco events and makes no claim to be one: no citywide denominator exists. Museum, market and festival programmes are over-represented; library programmes, most theatre, weekday civic meetings and many neighbourhood events are under-covered. No claim is made about popularity, attendance or current ticket availability.

Rows are labelled field-level (values transcribed from the page by a researcher), page-level (page and event name re-confirmed by the automated pass, values not re-transcribed) or flagged (unresolved note). The After Dark themes are dated occurrences of one weekly series and are not double-counted as series.

No continuous refresh is claimed. CI checks data structure, evidence references, local links and the live status of each cited URL; it cannot establish factual truth.
