What the data shows — and what it cannot

  • It is a festival city with a thin weekly layer. 120 annual entries against 22 weekly ones. The weekly layer is mostly farmers markets, museum nights and standing park programmes.
  • Blank is the honest default. 205 rows carry review notes. Where an organizer has not published a next date, the date cell is blank rather than extrapolated — a recurring pattern is not a confirmed future instance.
  • Fifteen entries are dated 2026 editions that have already happened (for example Tulip Day in March and the Opera Ball gala). They are retained as dated records, not shown as upcoming.
  • There is no citywide denominator. No official source publishes a complete count of San Francisco events, so this register cannot claim exhaustive coverage. Known gaps: library programmes, most theatre, neighbourhood block parties, sports beyond the Giants, and weekday civic meetings.
  • Two registers were reconciled. The 86-row register (sessions 3a19e72d/5a85baf2) and the 167-row inventory (session 4f47c142) were merged with an explicit map; overlapping rows were reconciled rather than counted twice.
