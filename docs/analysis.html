Findings

1. Annual festivals dominate; genuinely weekly events are few. 44 of 61 entries repeat annually, while only 4 run weekly and 3 monthly. San Francisco's public event calendar is a festival calendar, not a weekly-rhythm calendar — the weekly layer is almost entirely farmers markets plus the Golden Gate Park Band.

2. Roughly a third of the register has no published next date. 22 of 61 entries (36%) have a confirmed event and cadence but no next-edition date on the organizer's own site. This is concentrated in autumn: after their 2026 editions, organizers such as Hardly Strictly Bluegrass, Litquake, Sunday Streets and the Greek Food Festival had not posted 2027 dates. Any list that shows 2027 dates for those events is extrapolating, not reporting.

3. Official pages frequently disagree with each other. 14 entries carry a genuine irregularity. The recurring patterns are:
· Hours differ between two City/organizer pages — Alemany Farmers Market (7am-2pm on sf.gov vs 7am-3pm in the City's own market rules PDF); Heart of the City Farmers' Market (two different hour sets on the operator's own site); Castro Night Market (5-9pm vs 5-10pm); Chinatown Autumn Moon Festival (11am-5pm from the organizer vs 11am-6pm in the SFMTA advisory).
· Stale official pages — the Haight Ashbury Street Fair's About page still says the fair is held "every second Sunday in June" while the 2026 fair was held September 20; the City's sf.gov Filipino American History Month page still lists the previous FilBookFest edition; Castro Street Fair, Union Street Festival and Pistahan pages still show 2026 content.
· Moved or rescheduled editions — the San Francisco Opera Ball was rescheduled from September 12 to November 12, 2026 because of orchestra labor negotiations.

4. Third-party calendars are the main source of wrong SF event lists. Four items were excluded because they are repeatedly listed as San Francisco events when they are not, or no longer happen: How Weird Street Faire (official site: "The Faire is done"), the Ghirardelli Chocolate Festival (no edition on the venue's own events page since 2018), the Great Dickens Christmas Fair (Cow Palace, Daly City) and Super Bowl LX / FIFA World Cup 2026 / 49ers games (Levi's Stadium, Santa Clara). None of these would survive an official-source check.

5. Geography matters. Several large "SF" draws are held outside the City and County of San Francisco — Levi's Stadium, the Cow Palace, Oakland and Berkeley venues. The register only includes events whose official page places them inside the city; for a few multi-venue events (SFFILM, Frameline, Noise Pop) the festival is San Francisco-based even where individual screenings or shows are in neighbouring cities, which is noted on those rows.

6. Source concentration. Verification leaned on 43 distinct official domains. City sources (sf.gov, sfrecpark.org, sfmta.com, sfpl.org, sfport.com) cover the civic and parks layer; festival organizers cover the rest. Where a City page and an organizer page disagreed, the organizer page was treated as authoritative for the event's own schedule and the disagreement was flagged.

Limits of this register

This is a register of the events that could be confirmed on an official page on 2026-10-18. It is not a claim to be exhaustive: smaller neighbourhood fairs, single venue concert series, museum openings and library programmes exist in San Francisco that this pass did not reach. Adding them is additive — each needs its own official source link, and the schema in data/events.json already supports it.

Every row is sourced to an organizer, City or venue page and carries the date it was checked. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and marked unverified rather than inferred. Discrepancies between official sources appear on Flagged for Review.
