How the register is organised

Recurring — weekly. Events with a fixed weekday pattern that run all year (farmers markets, the Golden Gate Park Band's Sunday concerts).

Recurring — monthly. Events with a fixed monthly pattern (Downtown First Thursdays, Castro Night Market, the Fort Mason night market).

Recurring — annual. Festivals, parades, seasons and races that return once a year — the bulk of the register.

Special / dated. One-off or single-edition events with a specific date rather than a repeating pattern.

Reading the status column

verified — event, cadence and date confirmed against the linked official page.

date unverified — the event itself is confirmed on an official page, but the organizer has not published the next edition's date. The date cell is left blank rather than estimated.

runs on this pattern — a continuous series (a weekly market, a full concert season) whose official source defines the pattern rather than a single next date.

flagged — a genuine irregularity: official sources disagree, an official page is stale, or a value could not be pinned down. Full detail on Flagged for Review.

Every row is sourced to an organizer, City or venue page and carries the date it was checked. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and marked unverified rather than inferred. Discrepancies between official sources appear on Flagged for Review.
