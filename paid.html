Paid, kept separate on purpose

This project focuses on free events: the landing page and the Free page carry free entries only. Priced events are still collected and organized — here, in their own section. Every entry below quotes a price, ticket or entry fee on its official page, and the exact wording is shown in the Admission column, including member pricing, play-up or admin fees where the page states them in its price line. Entries whose official page says nothing about price are not here: “Not stated” means unverified, not paid. Dated entries come first, nearest date first; the rest follow alphabetically.
